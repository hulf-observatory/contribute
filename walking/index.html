Contribute · Walking

Tell us about walking here

Is there a footpath, can you walk on it, is it blocked, shaded, lit? Can you cross the road safely? Tell us about one spot or crossing in a couple of minutes, or about a whole walk.

What would you like to do?

Safety first. Watch for traffic and stand somewhere safe; you don't need to be right at the spot. Skip anything that doesn't feel safe.

Collect points on the leaderboard: Join as a contributor · Log in

Make up a nickname (not your real name) and a 4-digit PIN. The same nickname works for bus stops and footpaths.

Contributing as · Leaderboard · Log out

This is a public data exercise, not a complaint service. What you send is shared openly so residents, researchers and city offices can see the same picture. We don't pass it on as a complaint or follow up on it. To get something fixed, contact the department responsible directly.

No names, no email, and we don't keep IP addresses. You can take part without a nickname too.
