Walking map

Main roads (highways, arterials and sub-arterials) are cut into stretches of up to about 500 m, junction to junction. Each side of a stretch has its own profile, and so does each junction where main roads meet: the first person to describe it builds the profile, and everyone after confirms it or changes what's different. Spots on other streets show as single dots. Pick one thing to show at a time, then tap a road side, a junction or a dot.

Tap a side of a road, a junction or a dot to see what people found there. Grey road sides have no profile yet.

Walked along one of these recently? Tap it and press Still like this, or say if something looks wrong. Checks keep the profiles current. Contributors who log in also appear on the leaderboard.
