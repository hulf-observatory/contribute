How we handle what you send

  • A public data exercise, not a complaint service: what you send is shared openly; we don't pass it on as a complaint or follow up on it.
  • No real names or email, and we don't keep IP addresses. A nickname for the leaderboard is optional.
  • Best on a phone, at the place itself, from your phone's location (you can move the point up to 100 m, so you can stand somewhere safe). Not there, or on a laptop? Mark a place you know on a map: it is shown as "from memory" (a flood: one you saw in the last 7 days). Tankers can be reported from anywhere.
  • Your location is used to show what is near you. We keep the place of what you describe, never your route.
  • Photos are made smaller and their hidden location data is removed on your phone, before sending.
  • Answers are shared as open data under CC BY 4.0; photos are shown on the website but not in the download.
  • Want updates? Join the Observatory's WhatsApp community.
