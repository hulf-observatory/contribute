How we handle what you send

  • No names, no sign-in, and we don't keep IP addresses.
  • The forms are filled in on a phone. Flooding, bus stops and footpaths are reported at the place itself, from your phone's location (you can move the point up to 100 m, so you can stand somewhere safe). Tankers can be reported from anywhere. For bus stops, your location only finds the nearest stops; it is sent only if you say the stop is where you are.
  • Photos are made smaller and their hidden location data is removed on your phone, before sending.
  • Answers are shared as open data under CC BY 4.0; photos are shown on the website but not in the download.
  • Want updates? Join the Observatory's WhatsApp community.
