Tell us about a bus stop

Tell us what a bus stop is like: shelter, footpath, lighting, signs. It takes about two minutes. Anyone can update a stop later, so the picture stays current.

Are you at the bus stop now?

Details come from people standing at the stop, so they stay current. Come back when you're at a bus stop. Meanwhile, see the stops map.

Safety first. Watch for traffic and buses, and stand somewhere safe; you don't need to be right at the stop. Skip anything that doesn't feel safe.

Share your location?

Your phone will ask whether this page may use your location. We use it only to find the bus stops near you.

  • Nothing is saved until you press Send at the end, after you have checked your answers.
  • We save the bus stop you pick, not your name, phone number or IP address. Your location is saved only if you tell us the stop is really where you are standing.
  • Location is used to list the nearest stops and to check the stop is in the right place on the map.

Which stop are you at?

Each side of the road is a separate stop. Pick the one you are standing at.

Add a photo?

Optional. One photo of the stop helps others see it.

Step away from traffic before you take a photo. Shown on the stop's page. Try to keep people's faces and vehicle number plates out of it. Your phone makes the photo smaller and removes the location and camera details stored inside it.

Optional. For example "a shelter", "move the parked autos", "a light".
